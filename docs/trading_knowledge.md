# Trading Knowledge Base

A comprehensive guide to trading concepts, patterns, and strategies.

## 1. Dow Theory
Dow Theory is the foundation of technical analysis, based on the writings of Charles Dow.
**Key Tenets:**
1.  **The Market Discounts Everything:** Price reflects all available information.
2.  **The Three Market Trends:**
    *   **Primary Trend:** Long-term (Years). The "Tide".
    *   **Secondary Trend:** Intermediate corrections (Weeks/Months). The "Waves".
    *   **Minor Trend:** Short-term fluctuations (Days). The "Ripples".
3.  **Trends Have Three Phases:**
    *   **Accumulation:** Smart money buys.
    *   **Public Participation:** Trend becomes visible, public joins.
    *   **Distribution:** Smart money sells to latecomers.
4.  **Indices Must Confirm Each Other:** (e.g., Nifty and Bank Nifty should move together).
5.  **Volume Must Confirm the Trend:** Volume increases in the direction of the trend.
6.  **Trends Persist Until Reversal:** A trend is assumed to continue until a definitive signal proves otherwise.

## 2. Candlestick Patterns (Profitable)

### Bullish Reversal (Buy Signals)
*   **Hammer:** Small body near top, long lower wick. Rejection of lower prices.
*   **Bullish Engulfing:** Large green candle completely engulfs previous red candle. Strong buying pressure.
*   **Morning Star:** 3-candle pattern. Red candle -> Small Doji/Spinning Top -> Green candle. Transition from selling to buying.
*   **Piercing Line:** Green candle opens lower but closes above 50% of previous red candle's body.

### Bearish Reversal (Sell Signals)
*   **Shooting Star:** Small body near bottom, long upper wick. Rejection of higher prices.
*   **Bearish Engulfing:** Large red candle engulfs previous green candle.
*   **Evening Star:** Green candle -> Small Doji -> Red candle.
*   **Dark Cloud Cover:** Red candle opens higher but closes below 50% of previous green candle.

### Indecision
*   **Doji:** Open and Close are virtually the same. Market is undecided. Watch for breakout.

## 3. Support & Resistance (S&R)
*   **Support:** Price level where buying interest is strong enough to overcome selling pressure. "Floor".
*   **Resistance:** Price level where selling pressure is strong enough to overcome buying pressure. "Ceiling".
*   **Role Reversal:** Once Resistance is broken, it often becomes Support (and vice versa).

## 4. Chart Patterns

### Reversal Patterns
*   **Double Top:** "M" shape. Two peaks at similar level. Bearish reversal after uptrend. Entry below neckline.
*   **Double Bottom:** "W" shape. Two troughs at similar level. Bullish reversal after downtrend. Entry above neckline.
*   **Head and Shoulders:** Peak (Left Shoulder), Higher Peak (Head), Lower Peak (Right Shoulder). Bearish.
*   **Inverse Head and Shoulders:** Upside down version. Bullish.

### Continuation Patterns
*   **Triangle:** Converging trendlines.
    *   *Ascending:* Flat top, rising bottom. Bullish.
    *   *Descending:* Flat bottom, falling top. Bearish.
    *   *Symmetrical:* Both converging. Breakout direction determines trade.
*   **Flag & Pennant:** Sharp move (Poll) followed by small consolidation (Flag). Usually breaks in direction of Poll.
*   **Cup with Handle:** U-shaped cup followed by small downward drift (handle). Bullish breakout above handle.

## 5. Stock Selection Criteria (Intraday)
1.  **Liquidity:** High volume stocks (Nifty 50/100). Ensures easy entry/exit.
2.  **Volatility:** Beta > 1. Stocks that move enough to give profit.
3.  **Trend Alignment:** Trade with the sector and market trend.
4.  **Volume Spikes:** Look for abnormal volume in the first 15 mins.
5.  **Key Levels:** Stocks near yesterday's High/Low or daily S&R.

## 6. Trading Psychology & Qualities
*   **Discipline:** Following your plan strictly.
*   **Patience:** Waiting for the perfect setup (A+ setup). Does not chase trades.
*   **Risk Management:** Never risking more than 1-2% of capital per trade.
*   **Emotional Control:** Not getting huge ego from wins or depression from losses.
*   **Review:** Journaling everyday to learn from mistakes.

## 7. Strategies (Summary)
*   **Moving Average Crossover:** Fast MA crosses Slow MA. Simple trend following.
*   **MACD Trendline:** breaking trendline on MACD indicator often precedes price break.
*   **SuperTrend:** Excellent for trailing stops in strong trends.
*   **Bollinger RSI:** Buying at lower band + RSI oversold -> Mean Reversion.
